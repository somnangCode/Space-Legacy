                                    Build a mission step by step

                                    1. Rocket Click a bay to roll a rocket onto the pad.
                                    2. Spacecraft Fit what rides on top (and a rover, if you land).
                                    3. Suit For crews only: choose what they wear.

                                                Quick guide

                                                How to use Space Legacy

                                                1. Explore the Solar SystemDrag to turn, scroll to zoom. Click a planet, or pick it from the list, to learn about it.
                                                2. Watch a real missionOpen Missions and press Replay. Slow it down or speed it up with 0.5× 1× 2×.
                                                3. Real MissionsIn Missions: every real mission and its hardware, each with Replay mission.
                                                4. Free BuildIn Simulator, mix any rocket, spacecraft, suit and destination, then Launch. Mixes that never flew are marked fictional.
                                                5. Explore the surfaceWalk, drive and record what you find. It is saved in your Collection.
